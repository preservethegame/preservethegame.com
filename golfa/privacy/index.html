Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026 · Preserve the Game · Warrenton, Oregon, USA

What golfa is

golfa.. ("the App") is a paid membership app for golfers, operated by Preserve the Game ("we," "us"). This policy describes what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We built golfa as a private club, and we treat member data accordingly: we collect what the product needs to work, and nothing else.

What we collect

  • Account information. Email address, display name, member handle, and an optional profile photo.
  • Golf records you create. Rounds and hole-by-hole scores, handicap information (self-reported, or synced from the USGA GHIN system if you connect it), games and their results, highlights, course reviews and tips, bucket lists, and photos you attach to posts.
  • Financial records between members. The App tracks stakes, expenses, and who-owes-whom ledgers for games and trips you take part in. These are bookkeeping records only — golfa never holds, transfers, or processes money (see the Terms).
  • Location. If you grant location permission, we store your approximate location to power proximity features (matching you with nearby members via "Pull the Flag"). Your location is never shown to other members — only approximate distance relationships are used. You can decline or revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Device and notification data. A push-notification token if you enable notifications, and your notification preferences.
  • Subscription status. Your membership level, synced from Apple via our subscription-management provider. We never see or store your payment card details — payments are handled entirely by Apple.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell member data. To anyone, for anything.
  • We do not run advertising and do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not process payments or hold funds; settlement of amounts tracked in the App happens between members, outside the App.

Who can see what

Visibility inside the App follows your choices. Posts carry member-controlled visibility levels (from public-to-members through private). Financial records for a game or trip are visible only to its participants and to viewers a host explicitly adds with an on-screen consent step. Your location is never visible to other members. Note that "public" content is public to golfa members — the App has no anonymous public web presence for member content.

Service providers

We use a small number of infrastructure providers to run the App, each processing data solely on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), RevenueCat (subscription status management), Apple (payments, push-notification delivery), and Expo (app runtime and push-notification routing). If you connect GHIN, handicap data is exchanged with the United States Golf Association's GHIN service per their terms.

Data retention and deletion

You can delete your account at any time inside the App (Profile → Delete account). Deletion is permanent: your profile is scrubbed and your login, posts, photos, rounds, and network connections are destroyed. Records that belong equally to other members — the results of games you played and ledger entries between you and them — remain in their records, attributed to "Deleted member" with no personal information attached. Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription; manage that in iOS Settings.

Age requirement

golfa is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from minors; accounts believed to belong to minors are terminated.

Security

Member data is protected with industry-standard measures: encrypted transport, row-level database access controls, and server-side enforcement of visibility and membership rules. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting your personal information occurs, we will notify affected members as required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Most of these are available directly in the App (your records are yours to view and edit; deletion is built in). For anything else, contact us and we will respond within the timelines applicable law requires.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, we will notify members in the App. Questions: golfa@preservethegame.com.
